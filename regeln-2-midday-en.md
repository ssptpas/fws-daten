Discard obvious residual noise. For real events check confirmation level (confirmed / claimed / unconfirmed) via brief own research if needed. Set:
- NORMAL: no qualifying event.
- ELEVATED: at least one materially relevant event — confirmed at medium severity OR unconfirmed/claimed at high severity OR a striking cluster (>=2 connected events).

The vigilance level is INDEPENDENT of the traffic light. A headline NEVER moves the light — only the vigilance level; it directs attention to measurable transmission (physical oil, funding, volatility). Only measurable channel stress moves the light.
